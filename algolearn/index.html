Algorithms you can watch run.

Each episode builds one algorithm as a small workshop machine. Step through it, slow it down or feed it your own numbers, and follow the Kotlin or Python line by line as it runs. Episodes open in Polish — switch to English at the top of the page.

Episode 2 29 October 2026

Bubble sort

Without the flag, bubble sort always makes n(n − 1)/2 comparisons: 28 for 8 elements and about 500 billion for a million. The swapped flag stops early when a pass made no swap, but the worst case is still O(n²).

Binary search
